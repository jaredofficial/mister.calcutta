Who Am I

A Life in Chapters

"My ancestors made ordinary cloth vivid. I do the same — with a city."

Born and raised in Calcutta, Iftekhar Ahsan — known to most simply as Mister Calcutta — has spent over two decades excavating the stories that this extraordinary city buries in plain sight.

He founded Calcutta Walks in the early 2000s, one of India's first heritage walking tour companies. He then restored a crumbling 1920s North Calcutta mansion into Calcutta Bungalow. He followed this with Calcutta Rooms, Kalimpong Bungalow, and the Art Cafe — each a living love letter to heritage and place.

Today, through @mister.calcutta on Instagram, he reaches millions every month — delivering short documentary reels about forgotten histories, shot from the streets where they actually happened.

His ancestors were rangrez — cloth dyers from Rajasthan. Men who took something ordinary and made it vivid. Iftekhar does the same thing, with a city.

Beyond the City
When he isn't in Calcutta's lanes, he's on the Silk Road — with his wife and their 12-year-old twins.
The twins are unschoolers. Their classroom is the world — Iran, Uzbekistan, Japan, Australia, Thailand. Travel is not a holiday in this family. It is the education.
